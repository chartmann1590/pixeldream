✦ 100% on-device generation

Turn a short idea into a picture — right on your phone.

PixelDream is a private Android app that generates images without a server. Describe what you're imagining, and an on-device AI paints it for you. No account. No internet required after setup. Nothing uploaded, ever.

Built to be private, not just to feel private

Everything that makes your image yours — the idea, the prompt, the result — stays where you typed it.

🔒

Nothing leaves your phone

Prompts and generated images are processed and stored locally. They are never uploaded as part of generating an image.

✈️

Works with no signal

After the one-time model download, PixelDream creates images with Wi-Fi and mobile data both off.

✨

AI-assisted prompts

A small on-device language model can expand a short idea into a richer, more detailed visual prompt before it's painted.

🖼️

A gallery that's actually yours

Every image is saved to a private, local gallery you can revisit, export, or share on your own terms.

🎛️

Tune quality vs. speed

Choose generation steps and image size to trade detail for speed, depending on your device and patience.

🛡️

Built-in safety checks

On-device checks run before and after prompt enhancement, plus an in-app way to report anything that shouldn't have gotten through.

A calm, focused interface

Three screens: describe it, watch it come together, and revisit everything you've made.

How it works

Two small AI models, running entirely on your hardware.

1

Describe your idea

Type a short description of what you want to see — a subject, a mood, a style.

2

An on-device model paints it

PixelDream optionally expands your idea into a richer prompt, then a local diffusion model renders the image on your hardware.

3

It's saved, privately

Your finished image lands in your private gallery, ready to view, export, or share whenever you choose.
