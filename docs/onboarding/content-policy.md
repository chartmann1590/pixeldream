# PixelDream Content Policy

_Draft — needs legal review before shipping (see plan's open risks)._

PixelDream lets you generate images from text prompts using on-device AI models. To
keep the app safe and Play Store-compliant, you agree not to use PixelDream to
generate:

- Content sexualizing minors in any way
- Content designed to harass, bully, or threaten a specific real person
- Extreme violence, gore, or content promoting self-harm
- Content that infringes another person's intellectual property or likeness rights
- Any content prohibited by the licenses of the underlying models (Gemma Prohibited
  Use Policy, CreativeML OpenRAIL-M use-based restrictions)

PixelDream applies automated, on-device filtering to prompts before generation, and
lets you report any generated image directly from the app. Reported content is
logged for review.

Continuing past this screen means you agree to these terms.
