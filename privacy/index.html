PixelDream Privacy Policy

Effective July 20, 2026

PixelDream generates images on your Android device. This policy explains what leaves your device and why.

On-device content

Downloaded AI models, prompts, generated images, and generation settings are stored locally. PixelDream does not upload prompts or generated images as part of image generation. Content leaves the device only when you deliberately share or export it, or choose to include it in a feedback or offensive-content report.

Data collected by services

  • Firebase Analytics receives app interaction events, app/device information, and identifiers used to understand feature usage. Prompt text and generated images are not included in analytics events.
  • Firebase Crashlytics and Performance Monitoring receive crash reports, stack traces, diagnostics, app/device information, and performance/network measurements so defects and slow operations can be fixed. Prompt text and generated images are not deliberately attached.
  • Google Mobile Ads may process advertising identifiers, IP address, device/app information, ad interactions, diagnostics, and approximate location derived from network data to deliver, measure, prevent fraud in, and where consent permits personalize ads. PixelDream uses Google's User Messaging Platform for applicable advertising consent and privacy choices.
  • Feedback and content reports are optional. If submitted, the report text, optional name/email, selected image attachment, and optional diagnostics are sent through a Cloudflare Worker to the PixelDream GitHub issue tracker. The issue tracker is public, so submitted report content and attachments may be publicly visible. Do not include sensitive information.

Data is encrypted in transit. Firebase, Google Mobile Ads, Cloudflare, and GitHub process data under their own terms and retention practices. PixelDream does not sell personal information.

Your choices

You can manage applicable advertising privacy choices from PixelDream Settings. You can avoid optional feedback collection by not submitting a report. Clearing PixelDream's storage or uninstalling it removes its local settings, models, and generated images; exported copies are unaffected. To request removal of a submitted public report, open a support issue and identify the report number without reposting sensitive data.

Children

PixelDream is not directed to children under 13 and should not be designated as a child-directed app in Google Play. Do not use it to create content that exploits or sexualizes minors.

Changes and contact

Material changes will be reflected here with a new effective date. Privacy questions can be filed in the PixelDream issue tracker.
